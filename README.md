The objective of the project is to analyze the socio-economic impact of Artificial Intelligence on the workforce across diverse industries. It focuses on the following primary goals:

* Economic Assessment: To quantify salary fluctuations (before vs. after AI) and identify which sectors experience the highest financial growth or stability.

* Structural Categorization: To determine the proportion of roles that are Modified (45%), Unchanged (40%), or Replaced (15%) in the current automation landscape.

* Risk Profiling: To correlate Automation Risk with Education Levels, establishing whether higher degrees act as a defensive barrier against job displacement.

* Strategic Upskilling: To statistically prove that targeted training acts as a "Safety Net," shifting at-risk employees into enriched, high-value modified roles.
